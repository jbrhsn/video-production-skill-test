# Editorial intelligence

Analysis creates evidence and candidate decisions before editing. Keep measured observations such as silences, word boundaries, stream changes, and detector confidence separate from editorial judgments such as hook strength, tangents, retakes, or explanation opportunities.

Candidates identify their source ranges, reason, confidence, context requirements, and before/after playback window. They do not alter the editorial timeline until accepted by a producer or a delegated autonomous policy. Preserve meaningful pauses and intentionally repeated language unless the decision record explains why a change is appropriate.
