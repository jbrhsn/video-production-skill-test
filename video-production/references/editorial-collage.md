# Editorial collage

Editorial collage uses layered evidence and original graphics to explain an idea. Build every beat from a persistent world or texture, supporting context, and a current focus when those layers help the story. Do not flatten independently moving objects into a single image.

Process assets reproducibly. Preserve the original, record the crop/tone/mask/texture recipe, inspect alpha and margins, and retain provenance and rights. Use motion to show a relation, accumulation, comparison, cause, or result. Generic fades and drifting imagery are atmosphere, not explanation.
